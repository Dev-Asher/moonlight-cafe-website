Our Story

Moonlight Café opened in 2019 in a former print shop on Lumen Street, back when the block still went dark at seven and someone needed to keep the lamps on. We kept the old press legs as table bases, laid a mezzanine over the drying racks, and built the bar where the ink-mixing bench used to be.

Seven years later, the ritual hasn’t changed: coffee pulled late, a kitchen that refuses to close early, and a sofa corner claimed first come, first seated. Come for one cup and stay until the street is quiet again. The light is always on.
