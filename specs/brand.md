# Brand Specification — Moonlight Café

## Identity

- **Name:** Moonlight Café
- **Tagline (primary):** Coffee for the hours after dark.
- **Tagline (alternates):** "Stay a little longer." / "Open late. Warm always."
- **Category:** Late-night neighborhood café — coffee, small plates, and sweet things
- **Fictional location:** Northwind District, Cresthaven
- **Established (fictional):** 2019

## Short description

Used for the hero subline, meta description, and About copy (trim as needed):

> Moonlight Café is a neighborhood café in Northwind, Cresthaven, serving carefully
> brewed coffee, small plates, and something sweet — from the first pour of the morning
> to well after the rest of the street goes quiet. Come for the honey oat latte, stay
> for the window seats, the warm lamps, and a room that always has one more chair free.

## Tone of voice

**Cozy, modern, slightly premium, night-owl friendly.**

Do:
- Warm, specific, quietly confident — let the details do the selling
- Sensory language: "steamed oat milk", "wildflower honey", "burnt-top cheesecake"
- Short sentences. Second person used sparingly and gently
- Talk about time of day — this brand lives in the evening

Don't:
- Exclamation marks, emoji, or hype ("Best coffee EVER!")
- Clichés: "artisanal", "crafted with love", "fuel", "your daily grind"
- Discount or urgency language ("Don't miss out!", "Hurry!")
- Corporate filler ("solutions", "experience center")

These set the tone for the copy written during the build phase:

- **Open badge:** "Open now — kitchen until 10:00"
- **Closing-soon badge:** "Closing in 40 min — last orders at the bar"
- **Closed badge:** "Closed — opens Wednesday at 8:00"
- **Holiday closed badge:** "Closed for Christmas Day — back December 26"
- **Lively hint:** "Usually lively right now — great for people-watching, less for laptops."
- **Quiet hint:** "Quiet right now — the window seats are free."
- **Wi-Fi help:** "Free Wi-Fi. Ask the bar for the password — it changes with the moon."
- **Empty menu filter:** "Nothing here matches — try another category."
- **404:** "You've wandered off the map. The espresso is this way."

## Fictional-details rule (portfolio project)

Every business detail in this project is invented. Do not publish a real phone
number, address, business name, map pin, or social account.

- Phone uses the reserved **555-555-01xx** fictional range
- Email uses the reserved **.example** domain
- Address, district, and city are invented; postal code is invalid on purpose (00000)
- The map must be a placeholder — an illustration or generic-area embed, never a pin
  dropped on a real business
- Social handles are invented strings, linked to `#` until/unless replaced
